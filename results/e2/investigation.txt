Saved-state inspection and ranked explanations
1. Observed final-state failure: the class-producing ReLU head is inactive on
   the first 32 cached training images, inspected on CPU with dropout disabled.
   All 100 class maps are zero, giving uniform probabilities 0.00999999977648.
   Final preactivations range from -0.0990457088 to -0.0000671043163. All final
   class biases are negative. Every E2 convolution kernel has an L2 norm about
   2.24e-8 to 3.19e-8, versus roughly 9.32–15.45 in E0. The input-dependent
   variation is already zero at conv_02 at float32 precision for this sample.
   This establishes an inactive final head on the inspected sample and is
   consistent with optimization stalling early, followed by coupled-L2 decay
   of kernels receiving little classification signal. It does not establish
   when the head died or the causal sequence. Early checkpoints are unavailable;
   retained backups are only epochs 349/350. No tiny-batch retraining was run.
2. Vanishing gradients through increased depth is plausible but not established.
   Historical gradients, activations, and separate cross-entropy/L2 components
   were not logged. Final weights cannot reconstruct them. A dead final ReLU
   can block classification gradients itself; that differs from proving that
   depth-induced gradient attenuation caused the early failure. Finite end
   states do not rule out unlogged within-batch instability early in training.
3. Shared data/label or schedule mistakes are less supported: E0 learned using
   the same fingerprint/cache and unchanged model/data/training code. Training
   cache hashes and schemas validate; labels are float32 one-hot [50000,100],
   with exactly 500 examples per class. The builder uses 100-way softmax,
   CategoricalCrossentropy and CategoricalAccuracy; saved compile settings agree.
   Source inspection preserves image/label ordering and shuffles paired indices.
   Raw image/label alignment was not independently reconstructed, so an earlier
   data-preparation error cannot be absolutely excluded from these artifacts.
4. Nonfinite numerical divergence or an incorrect effective configuration has
   no positive evidence in the available artifacts. All observed values are
   finite, optimizer momentum is 0.9, optimizer weight_decay is null, and all
   convolution kernels use L2(0.0005), giving gradient coefficient 0.001.

Suggested conservative paper paragraph (researcher review required)
Under the registered 350-epoch configuration, the single-seed 18-convolution
E2 model failed to learn useful classification behavior: recorded training
accuracy remained near 1%, and the terminal loss plateaued near ln(100).
The prescribed learning-rate reductions did not restore learning. Inspection
of the final model on a fixed training subset found an inactive class-producing
ReLU head and nearly zero convolution kernels, consistent with stalled
optimization and subsequent regularization-driven shrinkage. These observations
do not identify the initiating mechanism or demonstrate vanishing gradients,
because historical gradients and intermediate activations were not recorded.
E2 also required more parameters and compute than E0; this is not a compute-
matched comparison. The result is one seed under one predetermined configuration,
not statistical evidence that increased depth generally causes failure. E2's
held-out test accuracy has not yet been measured.
